## Test House history

- `Test House`/`thetesthouse` was originally requested with rooms and a complete walkable interior.
- The interior became visually inconsistent: missing walls, color mismatches, stair openings, disconnected spaces, and exterior gaps.
- Later project direction favored clean exterior-only filler houses instead of continuing that complex interior.
- General lesson: establish a complete floor plan and circulation before adding furniture or detailed rooms.
