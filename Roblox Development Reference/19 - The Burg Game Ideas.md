# The Burg Game Ideas

## Build first

- [ ] **The Vibe delivery shift.** Pick up a takeout order at the counter. The phone shows the destination and house number. Deliver to the front porch and get paid in the existing Money wallet. Start with one order at a time and three houses. A porch interaction works with the exterior-only houses.
- [ ] **After-school basketball.** Start a 60-second shooting challenge at the gym. Show the score and personal best, and pay a small bonus when the player beats their record.
- [ ] **Lost and found.** A school NPC texts the player about a missing backpack. Follow three clues from school to Market Square and into the neighborhood, then return it for a one-time reward.

## Build once players have a way to earn cash

- [ ] **Bedroom customization.** Let players buy a poster, lamp, or desk decoration and place it in a preset spot in their room. Start with three slots and save selections per Story file.
- [ ] **Neighborhood reputation.** Give one recurring NPC a short chain of jobs. Finish a few and unlock a new route, better pay, or a different conversation.
- [ ] **Weekend neighborhood events.** Try a school gym tournament or a Market Square pop-up with a short activity and a decorative reward.

## Small features to build one at a time

- [ ] **Neighborhood directory.** Put a map board at Market Square that lists the school, The Vibe, Luxury, and the main streets.
- [ ] **House numbers.** Add readable numbers next to front doors so players can find addresses on foot.
- [ ] **Doorbells.** Give a few porches a working bell with a short cooldown. Some houses could play a muffled “Coming!” from inside.
- [ ] **Chalkboard specials.** Give The Vibe a rotating sign with three preset specials and a matching line from the cashier.
- [ ] **School announcements.** Play a short bell at the start and end of the school day, with an optional announcement on the hallway board.
- [ ] **Photo spots.** Put a small camera marker at a few landmarks and use the existing phone camera for neighborhood snapshots.
- [ ] **Bottle returns.** Place a few recyclables around the plaza and let players turn them in at one bin for a small cash reward.
- [ ] **Porch lights at dusk.** Switch on a handful of porch lights when the in-game sun goes down so streets feel lived in.
- [ ] **Neighbor greetings.** Give two sidewalk NPCs a short greeting and one local tip, like where to find the gym entrance.
- [ ] **Mailbox note.** Put one handwritten message in the player's mailbox with a tip about a nearby activity.
- [ ] **Crosswalk signals.** Add a walk button and a short countdown to one of the existing four-way intersections.
- [ ] **Bench interactions.** Add seats at a bus stop and outside The Vibe so players can sit while waiting for friends.

House numbers, the directory, and porch doorbells also lay the groundwork for the delivery shift.

## First delivery milestone

The first playable version is one counter, three marked porches, one phone message per order, and a cash payout. Track the active order on the server and award it once at the correct porch. Playtest whether a new player can find the destination from the phone message alone and earn enough across a short shift to buy something they want.
